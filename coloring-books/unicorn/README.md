# Unicorn coloring book (40 pages)

A 40-page coloring book, one black-and-white drawing per page. Theme: cute (kawaii) unicorns.

**Personal use only.** The drawings in `candidates/` come from free coloring sites that allow personal use only. Do not sell or publicly share the book. Each image's origin is listed in `sources.csv`.

## Folders

| Path | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `references/` | The 5 drawings that set the style: girl in a bucket hat and sunglasses with a unicorn, with hearts, stars, flowers and rainbows |
| `candidates/` | 36 drawings found online that match the references |
| `sources.csv` | Site, image link and listing page for each candidate |
| `preview.png` | All 40 planned pages on one sheet |

## Page plan

| Pages | Source |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | `references/ref-01` to `ref-04` |
| 5–40 | `candidates/01` to `36` |

`ref-05-making-jam.png` is a spare. It has no unicorn, so it can replace any page if wanted.

The candidates are grouped by theme:

- 01–07: friends (a princess, a fairy, a reindeer, a bunny)
- 08–12: birthday party
- 13–20: sweets (cupcakes, ice cream, donuts, candy)
- 21–29: sky (moon, rainbow, clouds, stars)
- 30–33: garden and castle
- 34–36: holidays (Christmas, Halloween)

## How the candidates were chosen

- Black and white line art only, no colored images.
- A cute style close to the references: big eyes, a happy face, a background full of hearts, stars, clouds, flowers or rainbows.
- Print quality. 34 pages are 2400–2550 × 3100–3300 pixels (letter size at 300 dpi). Pages 02 and 03 are 1500 pixels wide.
- Sites with small images only (306 × 396 or 612 × 792 pixels) were left out.
- Pages showing characters from other brands (Hello Kitty, Stitch, Peppa Pig, Minnie, My Little Pony) were left out.
